# Explicit Product community rights and whole-Product revocation

Current subscription commerce and course terms follow [ADR 0037](0037-products-without-subscriptions.md)
(owner decision 2026-10-20). Its explicit supersessions take precedence over the historical decisions below.


The owner accepted #1442–#1444 together on 2026-10-14. A Product and Support no longer imply a
community benefit for new purchases. This supersedes that implication in ADR 0022 and ADR 0024;
their capability-package and Telegram delivery boundaries remain valid.

Telegram Membership owns the Product-to-group association. The first catalogue entry is the
existing group, `primary`; multiple Products can use it. Association grants no access. Group
admission combines valid community rights of associated Products and preserves historical unscoped
rights. A second group requires a provider contract that can address that group; configuration does
not pretend the current singleton provider can do so.

Existing grants retain their previously implied community benefit through a migration. New price
snapshots mark explicit community policy. Historical snapshots remain immutable and continue to
produce their original benefits when fulfillment recovers. The prospective legal edition remains
an owner decision in #1464; this change does not publish legal documents or open a real Offer.

A Product revocation records exclusions against its current grant identifiers instead of modifying
Billing-owned grant revisions. That avoids racing a refund or fulfillment and preserves other
Products of a broad grant. A later independent grant can establish new access. Refunds remain
purchase-scoped. Both paths preserve financial history and learning activity.

Reading Activity records when learning starts for a Product. This fact survives removal of a lesson
from the current programme, so completed onboarding does not reappear merely because content changes.
