# Platform UI catalog

Storybook shows the platform as production renders it today, for people and agents. Start with the
smallest layer that answers the question at hand:

1. **Foundations** explain the visual language: tokens and typography from `app/globals.css`.
2. **Components** document bounded controls, states, props, and accessible behavior.
3. **Patterns** show components collaborating around a reusable product responsibility.
4. **Pages** show every production route in its shell, with the states the route really has.

Proofs and prototypes stay on their `prototype/*` branches. The catalog keeps only what the product
still shows; Git history and the issue keep earlier decisions.

## Story contract

Each story captures one meaningful use case, explains why it exists, uses realistic Russian
product content, and asserts its critical behavior in a `play` test. Controls are for exploring
supported props; they are not a substitute for named states. Page stories are checked at 390 and
1440 wide and keep the same information hierarchy at both widths.

A story shows the production module in its production environment: a page-level story wraps it in
the shell of its route, a component story uses the containers its real page gives it, and neither
re-creates page markup of its own. `src/storybook/story-environment` owns those wrappers. When a
story and the application disagree, the application is the reference and the story changes.

A pull request that adds or changes a surface updates its stories in the same pull request.

## Routes without a page story

Every route under `apps/web/app` has a page story, except:

| Route                                                               | Reason                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/authoring/communications`, `/authoring/communications/broadcasts` | The layout answers 404 since #419: broadcasts and funnels moved to the Telegram bot (#396).                                                                                                                         |
| `/authoring/playlists`, `/authoring/playlists/[productId]`          | Legacy route aliases share `src/_pages/content-collections`, the owner of this surface, with `/authoring/products` and `/authoring/products/[productId]`. Production links and page stories use the Product routes. |

`/authoring` has no page yet: until the Overview of #1719, `next.config.ts` redirects it to
`/authoring/products`. The admin shell around every authoring page is shown in
`Patterns/Каркас админки`; the shared admin list modules, the person label and the people search
live under `Components/Админка`.

`/payment/checkout` resolves an Offer and redirects to `/products/<slug>/buy`.
Its guest sign-in and signed-in denied states are shown in `Pages/Payment/Checkout`;
the redirect itself has no rendered page.

The product page and the «Продажи» section of #1718 are shown in:

- `Pages/Authoring/Редактор продукта` with the tabs «Программа», «Описание и витрина», «Продажа»
  (price, sale state and the product's payment links), «Ученики» and «Сообщество и активация»;
  the tab modules alone are `Features/Product/Продажа продукта`,
  `Features/Product/Сообщество и активация`, `Pages/Authoring/Продукт/Ученики` and
  `Pages/Authoring/Продукт/Выдать продукт`.
- `Pages/Authoring/Платежи`, `Pages/Authoring/Платёж` and `Pages/Authoring/Платёж/Email заказа`
  for `/authoring/sales/payments` and one payment.
- `Pages/Authoring/Sales/Payment links` with `Components/Админка/Продажи/Создание ссылки на оплату`,
  `Pages/Authoring/Sales/Discounts` with `Components/Админка/Продажи/Форма скидки`, and
  `Pages/Authoring/Sales funnel report` for `/authoring/sales/funnel`.
- `Components/Админка/AdminConfirmDialog` and `Components/Админка/Выбор из длинного списка`, the
  confirmation and long-list choice these pages share.

The former `/authoring/billing` and `/authoring/sales-funnel` have no page: `next.config.ts`
redirects them to `/authoring/sales/payments` and `/authoring/sales/funnel`.

## Agent workflow

When Storybook is running, inspect the catalog and rendered Docs at `http://localhost:6006`. Read
the component documentation before editing UI, preview every changed story, and run
`pnpm test:storybook` and `pnpm build:storybook` before handing work back. JSDoc, story
descriptions, controls, and hierarchy should make intent recoverable without opening
implementation files.
